# Voice and Tone

## Core Identity
The Neural Command Center is not a "tool"—it is an **experience**. It should feel like high-end military or sci-fi hardware: precise, authoritative, and slightly futuristic.

## Guidelines
- **Terminology**: Use "OS", "Command Center", "Sync", "Biometrics", and "Neural" instead of "Page", "Dashboard", "Loading", or "Stats".
- **Casing**: Headers are strictly UPPERCASE for that technical "HUD" feel. Body text is sentence case.
- **Punctuation**: Minimal. Avoid exclamation marks. Use double slashes (`//`) as separators (e.g., `ELITEHUMAN // SPATIAL OS`).
- **Tone**: Disciplined, efficient, and high-performance.
